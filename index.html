Safe Food Handling Protocol

Food Safety & Quality Assurance

To ensure all food donated through ResQBites is safe and fresh, all donors must adhere to these standard hygiene guidelines based on regulatory food standards.

1

Preparation & Storage

  • Food must be packaged in clean, food-grade containers.
  • Keep hot food above 60°C or chill cooked food quickly below 5°C.
  • Keep all food items strictly covered at all times.
2

Time Limit & Shelf-Life

  • Cooked meals must be posted within 2 hours of preparation.
  • Perishable items must be picked up within a 4-hour window.
  • Never donate food stored overnight without refrigeration.
3

Inspection & Transport

  • Conduct visual and smell checks prior to releasing food.
  • NGO volunteers must use insulated collection bags/boxes.
  • Discard any items showing signs of spoilage or foul odor.

Donor Liability & Safety Checklist

What items CANNOT be donated?

Partially consumed food, unpasteurized dairy, raw meat products stored at room temperature, or any dish containing raw seafood.

How is food quality verified before distribution?

NGO volunteers complete a standard 3-point check (Temperature, Odor, Packaging Seal) upon pickup before signing the digital handover receipt.
